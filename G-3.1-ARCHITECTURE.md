# World Builder G-3.1: The Petroglyph Engine
**Finalized Vision & Technical Blueprint**

This document outlines the architecture for the G-3.1 World Builder. It has evolved from a manual SVG-placement tool into an AI-driven "Creative Engine" meticulously designed to animate the 300-line epic "Historia de la Isla".

## Core Philosophy: The Math of the Poem
The engine does not just read words; it reads the *fractal mathematics* of the 300-line poem.
*   **The Structure:** 3 Eras (Pasado, Presente, Futuro) $\rightarrow$ 3 Poems per Era $\rightarrow$ 3 Stanzas per Poem $\rightarrow$ 11 Lines per Stanza.
*   **The Engine Translation:** This structural rhythm dictates the pacing of the video. The UI score, the camera movements, and the scene length are directly derived from the character count and line structure of the poem (e.g., the 5-1-5 line rhythm in the Futuro acts as precise camera direction).

---

## The 4 Pillars of the G-3.1 Architecture

### Pillar 1: The Petroglyph Dictionary (The Art)
We are abandoning highly detailed, static clip-art. The engine generates visuals entirely through code (using `roughjs` for an organic, vibrating pencil aesthetic).
*   **The 15 Core Shapes:** We only need $\sim$15 primitive, reusable symbols (The Stick Figure, The Sun, The Wavy Lines, The Ship, The Stacking Squares).
*   **The Rendering Mode:** The user can use buttons to switch the engine between two modes:
    *   **[Literal]:** Uses character shapes (e.g., a stick figure bending under weight).
    *   **[Abstract]:** Uses pure geometric energy (e.g., a circle vibrating and shattering to represent cancer or chaos).

### Pillar 2: The 7 Archetypes (The Color System)
To prevent the screen from becoming a confusing jumble of lines over 300 history-spanning verses, the engine applies a specific color psychology derived from the 7 Archetypes/Gods:
1.  **Mars (The Force):** Crimson/Orange. Creation, fighting, beginnings.
2.  **Sun (The Light):** Gold/Amber. The hero, quests, mirror to reality.
3.  **Venus (The Beauty):** Emerald/Earth. Love, community, harmony.
4.  **Mercury (The Brains):** Cyan/Electric Blue. Reason, information, tools.
5.  **Moon (The Fairy):** Silver/Soft Violet. Emotion, nature, healing.
6.  **Saturn (The Judge):** Indigo/Charcoal. Discipline, structure, cold lessons.
7.  **Jupiter (The Queen):** Royal Purple. Solutions, expansion, triumph.
*   *Note: Giant Black & White shapes are reserved solely for immortal/conceptual forces (YaYá, Juracán, Dictators).*

### Pillar 3: The NLP AI Brain (The Pipeline)
*   **The Tool:** `@google/genai` (Gemini 2.5 Flash).
*   **The Function:** The poem goes in. Gemini analyzes the semantics of each line against the 7 Archetypes and the inherent emotional weight.
*   **The Output:** It returns the physical parameters to drive the glyphs: `Tension` (0.0-1.0), `Energy` (0.0-1.0), and `ArchetypeColor`.
*   *As the user changes a word (e.g., from "soplido" to "explosión"), the AI instantly recalculates the variables, and the UI reacts.*

### Pillar 4: The Score View (The UI)
We are replacing traditional timelines (minutes/seconds) with a UI built exactly on the poem's fractal.
*   **Three Main Columns:** Pasado, Presente, Futuro.
*   The user operates as a "Director." They do not drag keyframes. They review the AI's parameter generation, use the [Literal/Abstract] buttons, and let the visual engine perform the code.
*   **The Director's Note:** Every line has an optional text box where the Director can manually type a prompt (e.g., "Make the Sun explode into black squares"). This completely overrides the AI's default shape selection and forces a custom prompt to the engine for that specific beat.
*   **The Output:** `npm run render` produces a buttery-smooth video syncing the procedural animation to the rhythm of the text.

### Pillar 5: Living Typography (The Layout Strategy)
The primary layout rule of World Builder is that **Words are the protagonist**. The animation serves as "living punctuation" to the text, not a replacement for it. 
*   **Desktop-First Focus:** The engine initially targets a Desktop viewing experience. It renders a full 11-line stanza in the center of the screen. As the timing engine activates each line (like a bouncing karaoke ball reading along), the generative petroglyphs spawn directly *anchored* to the text (e.g., animating on top of or around the specific words).
*   **Mobile (Future Phase):** A vertical "Rolodex" view where a single massive line sits in the center and slides away as the next line appears, with animations occurring intimately around the words.
