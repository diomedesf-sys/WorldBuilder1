# World Builder G-3.1 Workflow Roadmap

Welcome to the new era of World Builder. This application is designed to take the 300-line epic "Historia de la Isla" and translate it directly into dynamic, parametric animations.

This roadmap explains your step-by-step journey through the tool, acting as the ultimate "Story Co-Pilot" for your fractal poetry.

## 1. The Source Code (Loading the Fractal)
* **What you do:** The app automatically ingests the 300-line poem.
* **The Magic:** The engine recognizes the mathematical architecture of your work (3 Eras x 3 Poems x 3 Stanzas x 11 Lines). It sets up the UI and the timeline scaffolding perfectly to match this structure. 
* **The Goal:** A synchronized, massive grid ready for directing.

## 2. The AI Assignment (The Brain)
* **What it is:** The engine iterates through all 300 lines of the poem.
* **The Magic:** Using the Gemini API, it analyzes the semantic meaning of every single line. It asks two questions:
    1. **What is the Tension and Energy?** (e.g., "dictator" = High Tension, "harmony" = Low Tension).
    2. **Which of the 7 Gods/Archetypes is driving this line?** (e.g., The Force, The Light, The Beauty).
* **The Goal:** An automatically generated JSON array containing the exact parametric math and color palette (derived from the 7 Gods) for the entire 300-line video.

## 3. The Screening Room (The Score View UI)
* **Where you go:** The main UI.
* **What you do:** You review the 300 lines. The timeline is not in minutes and seconds; it is three massive columns (Pasado, Presente, Futuro).
* **What you see:** For every line, you see the AI's translation: The exact Petroglyph selected, the Energy spike, and the Archetypal Color.
* **The Goal:** The ultimate high-level Director's review layer to see the entire rhythm of your civilization at a glance.

## 4. The Directorial Overrides (The Human in the Loop)
* **What you do:** You find a line where the AI got the tone wrong, or you want a different visual impact.
* **How it works:** 
    * **[Literal] and [Abstract] Buttons:** If the AI used the "Sick Person" glyph for Cancer, but you want pure, vibrating geometric chaos, you hit the "Abstract" button. The engine instantly recalculates to use shattering circles instead of human forms.
    * **Shift the God:** If the AI assigned a line to "The Judge (Saturn)" but you meant it as "The Queen (Jupiter)", you switch the archetype. The stick figures instantly shift from dark indigo to royal purple.
    * **The Director's Note:** If you want absolute, custom control, you click `[ + Add Note ]` and type a specific prompt. The engine abandons its default petroglyph logic and generates a custom scene based entirely on your typed direction.
* **The Goal:** Absolute control over the emotional narrative without dragging a single keyframe or X/Y coordinate. 

## 5. The Render (The Masterpiece)
* **Where you go:** The terminal
* **What you do:** Run `npm run render`
* **The Goal:** Output a buttery smooth, parametric, "Living Typography" video. The text of the poem is the absolute protagonist, locked to the center of the screen, while the hand-drawn vector petroglyphs animate directly on top of or around the words (similar to a karaoke bouncing ball) as they are spoken. **(Focusing on the Desktop Stanza layout first, and the Mobile Rolodex layout second).**
