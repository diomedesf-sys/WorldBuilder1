# The G-3.1 Petroglyph Dictionary

This is the core visual alphabet for translating the 300-line "Historia de la Isla" into a procedural animation. We rely entirely on primitive geometry to convey massive historical and emotional weight.

By using these 15 core symbols in different combinations and driven by varying `Tension` and `Energy` parameters, we can tell the entire story from YaYá to the Mega-Modern Man.

---

## I. Human & Spiritual Entities

### 1. El Humano (The Human / Stick Figure)
* **Geometry:** A circle head, a vertical spine line, and jointed lines for arms and legs.
* **Usage:** Represents the Taínos ("gente desnuda"), Liborio, the Mega-Modern Man, and the "primera familia".
* **Parametric Reaction:**
    * *High Tension:* Shoulders bunch up, arms bend aggressively, knees lock. (e.g., Slaves working day and night).
    * *Low Tension:* Smooth curves, relaxed posture. (e.g., Taínos in harmonía).

### 2. La Multitud (The Crowd / Society)
* **Geometry:** Multiple instances of "El Humano" clustered together.
* **Usage:** Represents the Naborías, the mixed races ("fuimos una sola raza"), the rebels of '65, the people.
* **Parametric Reaction:** 
    * *High Energy:* Forms vibrate chaotically, overlap, and move rapidly (e.g., the battle of '65).
    * *Low Tension:* Organized, equally spaced, moving in unison (e.g., returning to the Cacicazgos).

### 3. El Ser (The Divine / YaYá)
* **Geometry:** A perfect, large circle. Empty or glowing.
* **Usage:** The beginning of creation, harmony, the spirit.

### 4. La Sombra (The Dictator / The Threat)
* **Geometry:** A massive, dark, jagged block or vertical monolith towering over the other glyphs.
* **Usage:** Represents Juracán, the Mercenaries, Trujillo ("reino de terror").

## II. Nature & Elements

### 5. La Ola / El Agua (The Water / The Sea)
* **Geometry:** Two or three parallel wavy lines (`~`).
* **Usage:** The water from YaYá's jar, the ocean Columbus crossed, the sea around the island.

### 6. El Sol / Fuego (The Sun / Fire)
* **Geometry:** A circle surrounded by radiating straight lines.
* **Usage:** "Con el sol se despertaban", the explosion, the burning of food in the modern era.
* **Parametric Reaction:** High energy makes the rays rapidly stretch and vibrate.

### 7. La Tierra (The Earth / Mountain)
* **Geometry:** A simple, solid triangle or a semi-circle anchored to the bottom of the screen.
* **Usage:** The conuco, the caves, "el monte Olimpo", the new country/campos.

### 8. El Aliento (The Breath / Wind)
* **Geometry:** Soft, curved, dashed lines moving horizontally.
* **Usage:** "Con un soplido" (The breath of creation), the passing of time.

---

## III. Objects & Technology

### 9. La Nave (The Ship)
* **Geometry:** A semi-circle base with a large triangle acting as a sail.
* **Usage:** The arrival of Columbus ("salido tres barcos").

### 10. La Estructura (The Structure / The City)
* **Geometry:** Rigid squares stacked on top of each other.
* **Usage:** Represents colonization, the division of the city in '65, the modern corporate world ("compañías farmacéuticas", "carteles y bancos").
* **Parametric Reaction:** They are rigid and unmoving, creating high tension against the organic shapes of nature.

### 11. La Herramienta / El Arma (The Tool / The Weapon)
* **Geometry:** A sharp, diagonal line intersecting a curved line (a sword, a gun, a tool).
* **Usage:** "Punta de un cañón", the trabuco, the '65 war, working the fields.

### 12. La Red (The Network / Technology)
* **Geometry:** A grid of connecting straight lines with dots at the intersections.
* **Usage:** The internet era, the Mega-Modern man, "todos conectados".

---

## IV. Abstract Forces & Transformations

### 13. El Cáncer (The Metástasis / Corruption)
* **Geometry:** A cluster of small, jagged, rapidly vibrating dots spreading randomly.
* **Usage:** "Nos parecemos al cáncer", corruption, the loss of values in the present.
* **Parametric Reaction:** This is purely an *Energy* driven shape. It starts as a clean circle (healthy) and splinters violently outward.

### 14. La Explosión (The Chaos)
* **Geometry:** Multiple jagged lines bursting outward from a central point.
* **Usage:** The creation of the universe, sudden wars, rapid technological shifts.

### 15. El Ciclo (The Cycle)
* **Geometry:** A circular arrow or a perfect ring that continuously rotates.
* **Usage:** "La vida es un ciclo", the return to the start, the new era, the post-data ("Nuestro futuro es ser uno").
