# World Builder G-3.1: Dominican Lexicon
**Cultural Context for the NLP Brain**

*This lexicon provides the Gemini AI Brain with the correct cultural and contextual meanings for specific terms in the poem. This prevents the AI from choosing literal (incorrect) geometric representations.*

### Era: Presente (1980s - 2000s)

*   **Jack Veneno:** A massive cultural hero and professional wrestler. Represents the battle of "good vs evil" played out as entertainment. (Themes: The Hero, Conflict, The Force).
*   **Engendro del Mal:** Jack Veneno's wrestling rival (Relámpago Hernández). Used metaphorically as a cartoonish villain.
*   **Pololo, Cuquín, Boruga, Freddy (Beras-Goico):** Iconic Dominican comedians and television hosts. They represent an era of shared, comforting domestic routine and laughter. (Themes: Laughter, Routine).
*   **Sorpresitas Popeye:** A nostalgic children's snack/toy. (Themes: Childhood, Innocence).
*   **Día de San Andrés:** A chaotic holiday tradition of throwing eggs (and talcum powder/water) at people in the streets. (Themes: Chaos, Play, Minor Conflict).
*   **Frigor (Paletas de Frigor):** An iconic ice cream brand. (Themes: Youth, Sweetness).
*   **Super 8:** A famous, thrilling roller coaster at an amusement park. (Themes: Excitement, Movement, Ups and downs).
*   **Quisqueya Park:** The legendary amusement park where the Super 8 was housed. (Themes: Danger, Thrill, Placing your life in someone else's hands).
*   **El Chavo (El Chavo del 8):** A legendary, comforting Mexican comedy show syndicated universally.
*   **Tres Patines:** A legendary, comforting Cuban comedy radio/TV show.
*   **Mundo sobre Ruedas:** A bustling roller-skating rink in Santo Domingo. (Themes: Circular motion, Youth, Fun, The Cycle).
